# Chapter 8 — Infectious Diseases
**TB, pneumonia, UTI, hepatitis, gonorrhoea/syphilis, malaria, HIV/OIs, SARS-CoV-2 | 12 hours**

## 1. Tuberculosis
Caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*.

### Symptoms
Persistent cough, fever, night sweats, weight loss; pulmonary disease may produce sputum/haemoptysis.

### Treatment principle
Drug-sensitive TB requires combination therapy using multiple anti-TB medicines according to the current national programme/guideline.

**Important adherence point:** incomplete treatment promotes drug resistance.

## 2. Pneumonia
Infection of lung parenchyma.

### Symptoms
Fever, cough, sputum, dyspnoea, chest pain.

### Management
Depends on setting and severity. Antibiotic choice depends on likely pathogen, local resistance, patient factors and guideline.

## 3. Urinary Tract Infection
### Symptoms
Dysuria, frequency, urgency; fever/flank pain suggests possible upper-tract involvement.

Treatment depends on site, severity, pregnancy status, resistance and culture when indicated.

## 4. Hepatitis
Inflammation of the liver. Viral hepatitis includes A, B, C, D and E.

**Exam point:** hepatitis B and C can cause chronic infection and chronic liver disease.

## 5. Gonorrhoea and Syphilis
Gonorrhoea is caused by *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*.  
Syphilis is caused by *Treponema pallidum*.

Both require appropriate diagnosis, guideline-based treatment and partner/public-health management.

## 6. Malaria
Caused by *Plasmodium* species and transmitted by infected female *Anopheles* mosquitoes.

### Important principle
Treatment depends on species, geographic resistance pattern and severity. Severe malaria is a medical emergency.

## 7. HIV and Opportunistic Infections
HIV progressively damages immune function if untreated.

### Management
Combination antiretroviral therapy (ART) suppresses viral replication and reduces morbidity/transmission risk.

Adherence and drug interactions are important.

## 8. SARS-CoV-2 / COVID-19
Treatment depends on severity and patient risk factors. Supportive care is important; selected high-risk patients may qualify for antiviral treatment according to current guidance.

## Antimicrobial Stewardship
### Goals
- Correct diagnosis
- Correct antimicrobial
- Correct dose/route
- Correct duration
- De-escalate when appropriate
- Avoid unnecessary antibiotics
- Monitor response/adverse effects
