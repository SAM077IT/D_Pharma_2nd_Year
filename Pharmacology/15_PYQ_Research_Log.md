# PYQ Research Log

## Sources used
### Government of Kerala — ER-2020 Model Question Papers
Official D.Pharm model-paper document based on PCI Education Regulations 2020. Its Pharmacology model uses 20×1, 10×3 and 6×5 marks and includes bioavailability, organophosphorus poisoning, antithyroid drugs, digoxin, benzylpenicillin, furosemide, antiplatelets, insulin, antiepileptics, morphine, antivirals, tetracyclines and biotransformation.

### HSBTE — Dec 2024 ER20-21T paper
Included bioavailability, pilocarpine, antidepressants, paracetamol, shock, streptokinase, bronchodilators, anti-ulcer drugs, quinine, penicillin, autocoids, sulfonamides, opioids, insulin, parenteral routes, cholinergic drugs, antihypertensives, antineoplastics, antifungals, estrogens and anti-TB drugs.

### Parul University — April 2024 ER20-21T paper
Included ADME, cholinergic/anticholinergic drugs and neuromuscular blocking agents as long-answer topics.

### Jharkhand Rai University — June 2025 model
Included bioavailability, IV route, neurohumoral transmission, cholinergic drugs, atropine, local anaesthetics, glaucoma, anticonvulsants, biotransformation, NSAIDs and antihypertensives.

### Important interpretation
The stars show recurrence/high exam relevance in the sources searched. They are **not a prediction** of the next paper.
