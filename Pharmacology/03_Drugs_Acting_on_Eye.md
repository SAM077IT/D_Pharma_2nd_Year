# Chapter 3 — Drugs Acting on Eye
## 2 hours | 🟡 QUICK

### ⭐ Questions
1. Define/classify miotics and mydriatics.
2. What is glaucoma? Name drug classes used in treatment.
3. Write a note on pilocarpine.

**Miotics:** constrict pupil. Example: pilocarpine.
**Mydriatics:** dilate pupil. Examples: atropine, tropicamide.

**Glaucoma drug classes:** prostaglandin analogues, beta-blockers, carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, alpha-2 agonists, selected miotics.
