## 2 hours | 🟡 QUICK

### ⭐ Questions
1. Define biological agents.

Biologicals are medicinal products derived from biological sources or produced using biological processes. Examples include vaccines, recombinant proteins, insulin and monoclonal antibodies.

Monoclonal antibody examples: trastuzumab, rituximab.
