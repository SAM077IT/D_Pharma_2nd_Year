### Anti-TB 🔴
Isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol.
**INH:** peripheral neuritis. **Rifampicin:** orange/red body fluids + enzyme induction. **Pyrazinamide:** hyperuricaemia/hepatotoxicity. **Ethambutol:** optic toxicity.

### Sulfonamides
Interfere with bacterial folate synthesis. Examples: sulfamethoxazole, sulfadiazine.

### Antivirals
Acyclovir is used for herpesvirus infections.

### Antifungals
Amphotericin B, fluconazole, clotrimazole.

### Anti-amoebic
Metronidazole is an important anti-amoebic/anaerobic agent.

### Anthelmintics
Albendazole, mebendazole.

### Antimalarials
Chloroquine where susceptible, quinine, artemisinin-based combinations.

### Antineoplastics
Alkylating agents, antimetabolites, plant-derived drugs, hormonal agents, targeted agents. Examples: cyclophosphamide, methotrexate, imatinib, trastuzumab.
