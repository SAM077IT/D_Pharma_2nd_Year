### Insulin
Preparations: rapid, short, intermediate and long acting. Uses: type 1 diabetes, selected type 2 diabetes, DKA. Major adverse effect: **hypoglycaemia**; other effects include weight gain and injection reactions.

### Oral hypoglycaemics
Metformin, sulfonylureas, DPP-4 inhibitors, SGLT2 inhibitors and others.

### Antithyroid
Carbimazole/methimazole; propylthiouracil. Inhibit thyroid hormone synthesis; PTU also reduces peripheral T4→T3 conversion. Important rare adverse effect: agranulocytosis.

**Hypothyroidism:** levothyroxine.
**Oxytocin:** uterine contraction and milk ejection.
**Corticosteroids:** hydrocortisone, prednisolone, dexamethasone; long-term effects include immunosuppression, hyperglycaemia, osteoporosis and adrenal suppression.
