### Anti-ulcer
Classes: antacids, H2 blockers, PPIs, mucosal protectives.
Examples: aluminium hydroxide, magnesium hydroxide, famotidine, omeprazole/pantoprazole, sucralfate.

**PPI mechanism:** inhibit H⁺/K⁺ ATPase in gastric parietal cells.
**Antiemetic:** ondansetron.
**Diarrhoea:** rehydration/ORS is fundamental; treatment depends on cause.
