### General anaesthetics
Examples: nitrous oxide, halothane (classical), propofol, ketamine. **Ketamine** is a dissociative anaesthetic.

### Sedatives/hypnotics
Benzodiazepines: diazepam, lorazepam, midazolam. Enhance GABA-A inhibition. Uses include anxiety, insomnia, seizures and sedation. Adverse effects: sedation, impaired coordination, dependence. Non-benzodiazepine: zolpidem.

### Antiepileptics 🔴
Examples: phenytoin, carbamazepine, sodium valproate, lamotrigine, levetiracetam, ethosuximide.
**Phenytoin:** blocks voltage-gated Na⁺ channels.

### Antidepressants
SSRIs: fluoxetine, sertraline. Other groups: SNRIs, TCAs, MAO inhibitors, atypicals.

### Antipsychotics
Typical: haloperidol. Atypical: risperidone, olanzapine.
