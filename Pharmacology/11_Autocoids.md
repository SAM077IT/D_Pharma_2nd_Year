### ⭐ Questions
1. Physiological role of histamine.
2. Role of serotonin (5-HT).
3. Role of prostaglandins.
4. Classify antihistamines.
5. Uses/adverse effects of antihistamines.
6. 5-HT antagonists.

**Histamine:** vasodilation, increased vascular permeability, bronchoconstriction, gastric acid secretion.
**H1 blockers:** diphenhydramine, chlorpheniramine, cetirizine. First-generation drugs commonly cause sedation.
**5-HT3 antagonist:** ondansetron.
**Prostaglandins:** roles in pain, inflammation, fever, gastric protection, renal blood flow and reproduction.
