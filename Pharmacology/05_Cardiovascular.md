### Antihypertensives
ACE inhibitors: enalapril. ARBs: losartan. CCBs: amlodipine. Diuretics: hydrochlorothiazide. Beta-blockers. Alpha-2 agonist: clonidine.

High-yield adverse effects: ACE inhibitors → dry cough; CCBs → ankle oedema; beta-blockers → bradycardia; diuretics → electrolyte disturbances.

### Anti-anginal
Nitrates, beta-blockers, calcium-channel blockers. **Nitroglycerin** is commonly given sublingually for rapid relief.

### Statins
Atorvastatin inhibits HMG-CoA reductase → lowers LDL.

### Digoxin
Increases cardiac contractility and affects AV conduction. Toxicity can cause arrhythmias, GI symptoms and visual disturbances.
