# Chapter 1 — Introduction to Pharmacotherapeutics
**ER20-24T | 8 hours**

## 1. Pharmacotherapeutics
Pharmacotherapeutics is the branch of pharmacy concerned with the use of medicines in the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of disease, with emphasis on rational and safe medicine use.

### Scope
- Selection of appropriate therapy
- Dose, dosage form and route
- Monitoring therapeutic response
- Identification/prevention of adverse drug reactions
- Identification of drug-related problems
- Patient counselling and adherence
- Individualized therapy

## 2. Rational Use of Medicines
WHO concept: patients should receive medicines appropriate to their clinical needs, in doses meeting their individual requirements, for an adequate period and at the lowest cost to them and the community.

### Elements
1. Correct diagnosis
2. Appropriate medicine
3. Appropriate patient
4. Correct dose and route
5. Correct duration
6. Correct instructions
7. Monitoring
8. Affordability

### Common causes of irrational use
- Polypharmacy
- Unnecessary antibiotics
- Incorrect dose/duration
- Self-medication
- Inappropriate prescribing
- Poor adherence
- Unnecessary injections

## 3. Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM)
EBM is the integration of:
- Best available research evidence
- Clinical expertise
- Patient values and preferences

### Basic process
Ask → Acquire → Appraise → Apply → Assess

## 4. Essential Medicines List (EML)
An EML contains medicines that satisfy the priority healthcare needs of a population. They are selected according to disease prevalence, evidence of efficacy/safety and comparative cost-effectiveness.

### Importance
- Promotes availability
- Supports rational prescribing
- Helps procurement
- Controls costs
- Supports standard treatment

## 5. Standard Treatment Guidelines (STGs)
STGs are systematically developed recommendations for managing common clinical conditions.

### Advantages
- Standardizes treatment
- Promotes rational drug use
- Reduces inappropriate prescribing
- Helps procurement and inventory
- Supports training and auditing
