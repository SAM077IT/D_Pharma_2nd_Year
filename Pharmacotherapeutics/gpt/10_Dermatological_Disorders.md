# Chapter 10 — Dermatological Disorders
**Psoriasis, Scabies, Eczema | 3 hours**

## A. Psoriasis
A chronic immune-mediated inflammatory skin disease.

### Features
Well-demarcated erythematous plaques with silvery scale are characteristic.

### Management
- Moisturizers/emollients
- Topical corticosteroids
- Vitamin D analogues
- Other topical/systemic/biologic therapy depending on severity

### Counselling
Disease is chronic and non-contagious. Treatment adherence and trigger management are important.

## B. Scabies
Infestation by *Sarcoptes scabiei*.

### Features
Intense itching, often worse at night; typical burrows may be present.

### Management
- Permethrin 5% cream is a commonly used treatment
- Treat close contacts where recommended
- Wash/appropriately manage clothing and bedding
- Avoid sharing personal items

## C. Eczema
A group of inflammatory skin disorders characterized by itching, dryness and inflammation.

### Management
- Regular emollients
- Avoid triggers/irritants
- Topical corticosteroids for flares when appropriate
- Treat secondary infection if present
