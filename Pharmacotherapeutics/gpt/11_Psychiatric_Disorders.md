# Chapter 11 — Psychiatric Disorders
**Depression, Anxiety, Psychosis | 4 hours**

## A. Depression

### Core symptoms
Persistent depressed mood and/or loss of interest/pleasure with associated cognitive, physical and behavioural symptoms.

### Management
- Psychological therapy
- Lifestyle/social support
- Antidepressants when indicated
- Combination treatment in selected cases

### Antidepressants
SSRIs are commonly used first-line medicines.

Important counselling:
- Benefit may take time
- Continue as prescribed
- Do not stop abruptly without medical advice
- Monitor for worsening symptoms/suicidal thoughts, especially early in treatment or after dose changes

## B. Anxiety Disorders
Excessive fear/worry with physical and psychological symptoms.

### Management
- Psychological therapies, especially CBT
- SSRIs/SNRIs where indicated
- Benzodiazepines are generally short-term/selective because of dependence and withdrawal risks

## C. Psychosis
A syndrome involving impaired reality testing, such as delusions and hallucinations.

### Management
Antipsychotic medicines + psychosocial interventions and treatment of underlying causes.

Examples:
- Risperidone
- Olanzapine
- Haloperidol

### Major adverse effects
Antipsychotics may cause extrapyramidal symptoms, sedation, metabolic effects and other class-specific adverse effects.
