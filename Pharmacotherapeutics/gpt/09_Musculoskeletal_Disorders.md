# Chapter 9 — Musculoskeletal Disorders
**Rheumatoid arthritis and Osteoarthritis | 3 hours**

## A. Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)
A chronic autoimmune inflammatory disease primarily affecting synovial joints.

### Features
Symmetrical joint pain/swelling, prolonged morning stiffness, fatigue and progressive joint damage.

### Management
- Patient education
- Exercise/physiotherapy
- NSAIDs for symptom relief
- Corticosteroids in selected situations
- Disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs), especially methotrexate, are central to disease control
- Biologic/targeted therapies in selected patients

### Methotrexate counselling
Usually given once weekly for RA, not daily. Folic acid is often co-prescribed. Monitoring is required.

## B. Osteoarthritis (OA)
Degenerative joint disease involving cartilage and other joint structures.

### Features
Activity-related pain, brief morning stiffness, reduced movement.

### Management
- Exercise
- Weight management where appropriate
- Physiotherapy
- Topical/oral analgesics/NSAIDs according to patient risk

## RA vs OA
| Feature | RA | OA |
|---|---|---|
| Main process | Autoimmune inflammatory | Degenerative/mechanical |
| Morning stiffness | Usually prolonged | Usually brief |
| Pattern | Often symmetrical small joints | Weight-bearing/common-use joints |
| DMARDs | Yes | No |
